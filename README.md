## How I approach the work

- **Start with the real problem.** My work around equipment keeps the ideas grounded.
- **Use AI as a tool, not a substitute for verification.** If it doesn't work, the fancy explanation doesn't count.
- **Keep people in control.** Privacy, clear permissions, and human review matter.
- **Be resource-conscious.** Efficient computing and longer-lasting equipment are goals worth measuring, not claims I can make without evidence.

I'm still learning, still testing, and still trying to make the next version better than the last.
